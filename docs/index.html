    Explore all eight planets and collect every crystal colour. Try Crystal Sprint to race for a medal! Use Quiz ⚡ to answer space and Scratch questions for extra supplies.

    • Autopilot: choose a planet or Mothership on the map. We return to the rocket, launch and land for you. Move or press Stop autopilot to take over.
    • Cloud islands: jump across the lit gaps. The rover stays on its platform. Your safety pack catches falls.
    • WASD / arrows: move and turn
    • Shift: run or rover turbo   Space: jump / flight boost
    • E: enter, exit, launch, dock or land
    • Right-drag: turn the camera   Wheel: zoom
    • Left-click: move to a point or select a target
    • In space: R rises, F descends
    • M: solar system   Esc: pause
    • Mouse wheel / + / −: ground-to-globe zoom
    • Ground / Globe: switch view   Spin: rotate the planet
