Downloaded original images from Wikimedia Commons mirror; no pixel edits.

Planet maps are equirectangular. Colors are artist-tuned from NASA imagery, with fictional fill for gaps; appropriate for an illustrative game. Earth clouds are grayscale JPG; use luminance as alpha on a white material. Saturn ring PNG is a radial profile, not a rectangular planet map; map x coordinate to ring radius. No 1K originals listed.
